# OMN-123 — Journal recovery can race an in-progress append

**Kind:** sdk-defect  
**Severity:** high  
**Status:** open  
**Packages:** `@totemsdk/storage`
**Observed versions:** @totemsdk/storage@0.2.3
**Origin:** Omnia adversarial audit — 26 November 2026 @ `c818f5e` (verified in source)

> reporter id OMN-121
**Source:** `Omnia adversarial audit — 26 November 2026, reporter finding OMN-121 (storage/src/journal.ts:250-287, 325-350)`

## Context

The journal advances its head before writing the entry, so recovery can read the missing entry as an interrupted append and roll the head backwards while another writer is mid-append. No lock spans recovery and an independent writer.

## Impact

Concurrent recovery and append can lose or duplicate journal entries that other packages rely on for crash safety.

## Reproduce

```bash
pnpm --filter @totemsdk/storage test
```

**Expected:** Append and recovery are mutually exclusive.

**Actual:** Recovery can roll the head back under a live writer.

**Evidence:**

- journal.ts:250-287 (head advance)
- journal.ts:325-350 (recovery)

## Where to fix


### Approach

Take an exclusive lock across recovery and appends, and only truncate entries proven abandoned.

## Constraints (do not violate)

- Do not lose a committed entry.
- Keep single-writer performance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Recovery during a paused append converges.
- [ ] A cross-process regression test covers the race.
