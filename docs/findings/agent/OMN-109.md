# OMN-109 — An interrupted signer response can release the signing guard

**Kind:** sdk-defect  
**Severity:** high  
**Status:** fixed  
**Packages:** `@totemsdk/omnia`
**Observed versions:** @totemsdk/omnia@4.0.0
**Origin:** Omnia adversarial audit — 26 November 2026 @ `c818f5e` (verified in source)

> reporter id OMN-107; fixed by classifying signer failures: a local signer or an explicit SignerRefusalError is a definite refusal and stays retryable, while an external signer that throws or times out seals the sequence, marks the intent ambiguous and surfaces SignerOutcomeUnknownError until reconciled
**Source:** `Omnia adversarial audit — 26 November 2026, reporter finding OMN-107 (omnia/src/sign.ts:323-354)`

## Context

The signing guard is sealed only after the signer returns. If an external signer produces a signature but the response is lost, the failure path abandons the attempt and releases the guard even though a signature may exist.

## Impact

Remote and asynchronous signers can be asked to sign twice at one sequence after a timeout.

## Reproduce

```bash
pnpm --filter @totemsdk/omnia test
```

**Expected:** An uncertain signing outcome stays locked until reconciled.

**Actual:** A thrown or timed-out signer is treated as "no signature".

**Evidence:**

- sign.ts:323-354

## Where to fix


### Approach

Distinguish a definite signer refusal from an unknown outcome; keep the guard sealed for the latter and require reconciliation.

## Constraints (do not violate)

- Do not treat a transport timeout as a refusal.
- Keep a definite pre-sign refusal retryable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A timed-out signer leaves the attempt sealed.
- [ ] Reconciliation can commit or abandon it explicitly.
