# OMN-072 — LSP fee admission and fee calculation ignore the flat fee and the provider's contribution

**Kind:** sdk-defect  
**Severity:** high  
**Status:** fixed  
**Packages:** `@totemsdk/omnia-lsp-provider`, `@totemsdk/omnia-lsp`
**Observed versions:** @totemsdk/omnia-lsp-provider@0.1.0
**Origin:** Omnia SDK audit — 25 November 2026 @ `9f882c8` (verified in source)
**Source:** `Omnia SDK audit — 25 November 2026, reporter finding OMN-070`

## Context

Admission compares only manifest.fees.openBps with request.maxOpenFeeBps, so a flat opening fee is never checked against the consumer's ceiling. Quote construction then computes openFee and leaseFee from request.localAmount (the consumer side) even though the provider is the party supplying the liquidity the lease is priced on.

## Impact

A consumer can be charged more than its stated maximum when the manifest carries a flat fee, and the provider's fee revenue is sized off the counterparty's deposit rather than its own commitment. Both directions of the commercial terms are wrong.

## Reproduce

```bash
pnpm --filter @totemsdk/omnia-lsp-provider test
```

**Expected:** The maximum-fee check covers the total the consumer will pay, expressed in token atoms, and the fee basis is explicit in the signed terms.

**Actual:** With openFlat set and maxOpenFeeBps 0, admission passes and the quote charges the flat fee. Changing remoteAmount does not change the lease fee.

**Evidence:**

- packages/omnia-lsp-provider/src/policy.ts:107-112 — only openBps is compared
- packages/omnia-lsp-provider/src/quote.ts:52-58 — openFee from openFlat/openBps, leaseFee from localAmount

## Where to fix

- `packages/omnia-lsp-provider/src/policy.ts`
- `packages/omnia-lsp-provider/src/quote.ts`

### Approach

Compute the full fee schedule for the request, compare its atom total against a maximum expressed in atoms, and state the fee basis (consumer contribution vs total channel value vs provider commitment) explicitly in the signed terms so the consumer can verify what it agreed to.

## Constraints (do not violate)

- Do not mix basis points and flat amounts in one unchecked comparison.
- Fees must be derivable from the signed terms alone.
- Changing a fee basis is a wire change: version the terms object.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A flat opening fee above the consumer's ceiling is refused at admission.
- [ ] The quote's fee fields match the schedule admission approved.
- [ ] The fee basis is explicit and covered by the terms hash.
